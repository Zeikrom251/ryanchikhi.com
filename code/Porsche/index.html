Who are we

Porsche AG is a German luxury car manufacturer founded in 1931 by Ferdinand Porsche. The company is headquartered in Stuttgart, and is best known for producing high-performance sports cars, SUVs, and sedans. Porsche is known for its iconic 911 model, which has been in production since 1964, and is widely considered to be one of the greatest sports cars of all time. Other notable Porsche models include the Boxster, Cayenne, and Panamera. Porsche is also known for its commitment to engineering and innovation, and its vehicles are renowned for their performance, handling, and build quality.
